# Cairn visual stories v2

New story selection starts from an environmental discovery, a demonstrated
mechanic, a visually funny or scary event, a verified fact, or striking scenery.
Conversations and relationship interpretations are no longer story foundations.
Write original hooks and explanations; do not fabricate factual secrets,
easter eggs, routes, lore or mechanics.

Every v2 plan declares its story basis and evidence-linked claims. Independent
source review and creative review must both accept `visual_story`. Research
records contain verified excerpts or labeled paraphrases and direct sources.
These reviews assess scripts and source evidence; final framing and audio need
separate checks against the actual exported file.

New clips use `tracked_cover`: original footage fills 1080×1920 with a uniform
scale and a horizontal crop driven by normalized subject positions. Keyframes
interpolate within a shot and can jump at actual camera cuts. There is no blur
background. Frame rate comes from the original, capped at 60; CRF 16 preserves
detail. A vertical crop necessarily excludes landscape content, so inspect the
important subject at the start, middle and end before narration production.

Garret remains the selected free HeyGen voice. Each complete script receives
one synthesis call; saved audio and exact timestamps are reused. Yellow active
word captions follow the complete spoken narration. Instrumental music must
have clearance; do not label a track trending without current evidence.

Preserve previous exports and source footage. Publish manually. Current
production files and checks are in `Releases/2026-11-15-visual-stories-v2`.
